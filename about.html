About FEDUC

Who We Are & What Drives Us

A professional development and service institution dedicated to promoting change for optimal efficiency of individuals, institutions and programs in general education and vocational skills training.

Our Vision

Promoting Change for Optimal Efficiency

FEDUC is a professional development and service institution dedicated to promoting change for optimal efficiency of individuals, institutions and programs related to general education and vocational skills training.

Our Mission

How We Pursue Educational Change

  • FEDUC is committed to providing professional services to national and international educational institutions through training, research and development of innovative programs.
  • FEDUC aims at developing innovative training techniques and teaching/learning materials for changing the existing educational practices in Nepal.
  • FEDUC undertakes tasks such as design, develop and test innovative training programs to develop quality human resources for educational institutions in Nepal and abroad.
  • FEDUC works for the welfare of underprivileged / disadvantaged children in different parts of the country, multiple intelligences and education services.
  • FEDUC serves as a forum for researchers, practitioners, policy makers and civil society relating to sustainable change and development in education, and provides services on project planning, monitoring and evaluation.
  • FEDUC brings out educational publications for disseminating innovative ideas and practices.
Our Objectives

Professionally Competent in Performing the Following Activities

1

Conduct large scale survey, research and evaluation studies and tracer studies geared toward bringing about appropriate changes and improvement in the education system and practices.

2

Facilitate appropriate changes in institutional mission, organization and management through consultancy and hands-on experience.

3

Create a conducive atmosphere for educational change through communication, advocacy and consultation.

4

Assist educational agencies, institutions and organizations in mobilizing their resources for institutional development and planning new directions for their programs.

5

Improve the quality, relevance and utilization of training programs by adopting participatory methodologies, modern technology, and gender and social inclusion perspectives.

6

Facilitate the interchange of ideas among professionals and academics working in the field of education for improved educational practices.

7

Assist in the design, implementation and use of Baseline, Monitoring and Evaluation (BME) systems.

8

Design, run and evaluate vocational programs, including job search and placement.
