Who We Are

Two Decades of Educational Change in Nepal

For more than two decades, FEDUC has contributed to vocational training for tradespeople such as electricians, plumbers and carpenters, delivered off-season vocational training, and provided teacher training in remote areas of Nepal.

Our team brings together researchers, trainers and development practitioners committed to strengthening education systems and building the skills that communities across Nepal need to thrive.

What We Do

Working Across Training, Research & Community Welfare

FEDUC's work spans vocational and teacher training, education research, and support for communities facing hardship across Nepal.

Vocational & Teacher Training

Hands-on training for electricians, plumbers, carpenters and other tradespeople, plus off-season training and teacher training programs delivered in remote areas of Nepal.

Research & Evaluation

Large-scale survey research, evaluation studies and tracer studies conducted across the fields of education and development in Nepal.

Community & Welfare Support

Support for orphans, prisoners' children, and socially and economically left-behind children, as well as unemployed youth across Nepal.

Emergency & Relief Response

Support for schools affected by the earthquakes in Nepal, and for students facing hardship during the COVID-19 pandemic.

Partner with FEDUC

Whether you represent an institution, a donor agency, or want to volunteer, we'd welcome the chance to work together on educational change in Nepal.
